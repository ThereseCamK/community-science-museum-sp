# Community Science Museum

Community Science Museum is a website where visitors can explore what the museum has to offer, including exhibitions, events, opening hours, and opportunities to get involved.

The website has dedicated sections for kids, teachers, and researchers, with information about what the museum offers to each group.

Visitors can also explore images from the museum in a separate gallery, which is accessible through the footer.

This project was created using HTML and CSS as my first semester project during my Front-End Development studies at Noroff.

## About the Project

The project was built using only HTML and CSS. I chose a colorful design to attract the attention of children while also appealing to teachers, families, and people interested in science.

The website has a hamburger menu on mobile devices and a navigation bar on tablets and desktops, making it easy to navigate between pages.

Visitors can quickly find general information about the museum, including ticket prices and opening hours, directly on the homepage. I also added links to other pages throughout the website, so visitors can easily find what they are looking for without having to use the main navigation.

This project was created as part of my first semester at Noroff, where I applied the knowledge and experience I had gained from previous courses.

The website is responsive and designed to work on mobile devices, tablets, and desktops.

## Features

### Homepage

- A short introduction highlighting the museum's commitment to making science accessible to everyone.

- General information about the museum.

- Quick access to exhibitions and events.

- Opening hours and ticket prices.

- A direct link to the Visit Us page.

### Exhibitions

Information about the museum's different exhibition areas:
- Robotics and AI
- Ecogoly
- Biology and Medecine
- Evolution
- Cosmology 

### Explore

The Explore page contains three interactive cards for different target groups:
- Kids

- Teachers

- Researchers

The cards flip when visitors hover over them, revealing additional information on the back.

Each card explains what the museum offers to its specific target group.
### Events

The Events page follows a similar layout to the Exhibitions page.

It presents information about events such as:

- Professor of Aeronautics

- Night in the Museum

- Energetica Exhibition

Each section contains information and an image, which is displayed on tablets, desktops, and larger mobile screens.

The sections also feature glowing gradient borders with an animated effect.

### Visit us

Visitors can find practical information about the museum, including:

- Address and a link to Google Maps.

- Ticket prices and opening hours.

- Accessibility information.

- Food and drink options.

- Information about the museum shop.

### Get Involved

The Get Involved page follows a similar design to the Exhibitions and Events pages, with glowing borders around the information sections.

It provides information about:

- Supporting the museum

- Volunteering

- Internship opportunities

###Gallery

- A separate image gallery showcasing the museum.

- Accessible through a link in the footer.

## Design

I focused on creating a colorful and eye-catching design that feels welcoming and appeals to different age groups.

I also wanted the website to have a clean and spacious layout, making the content easy to read.

To make the pages more visually engaging, I added interactive elements and animations that create movement and draw attention to different sections.
